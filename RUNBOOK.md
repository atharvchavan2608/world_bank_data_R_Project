# Runbook

One task per commit. After each task, run the pipeline in a fresh R process, review the rendered appendix and obtain a fresh-context check against the task gate and methods checklist. Update status, decisions and the run log before committing and pushing. Presentation and report deadlines provisionally fall on 29 and 30 November 2026, respectively.

| Task and target | Objective | Inputs → outputs | Report sections / methods | Done condition |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 0 · 21 Nov | Establish the project and reproducible entry point. | Brief, pitch, raw inventory, R/Pandoc/TeX → plan, runbook, tracking, setup, appendix, README. | All | Reviewer challenges design and sequencing, changes are logged, and a clean R process knits the setup-stage appendix. |
| 1 · 22 Nov | Rebuild national totals and audit coverage and measurement. | Raw SDMX, indicators guide, pitch and legal-origin dictionary → wave panel, cross-section, pitch reconciliation, attrition and origin audits. | Data and 1, 3 | Each source has unique totals, joins agree by economy/year, and the FIN14 anchor is checked against the union roster. Pitch statistics and year counts reconcile or have documented differences. Compare retained and dropped economies by observed FIN14, survey vintage and available geography. Verify indicator direction. |
| 2 · 23 Nov | Estimate simple and multivariate models and define inference. | Audited cross-section and variable definitions → models, fit, effects and tests. | Methodology, Results and 1–3, 5 | Models reproduce from saved inputs, nested comparisons use fixed samples, intervals and joint tests are shown, and the standardized court/bribery contrast uses their covariance. |
| 3 · 24 Nov | Diagnose fit and inference and apply remedies. | Baseline/pitch models → DW, BP, VIF, residuals, leverage and re-specified fits. | Methodology, Results and 2, 4, 5 | Diagnostics and hypotheses are stated, DW order sensitivity is shown, and a conventional/robust comparison quantifies the effect of the remedy. Document geographic coverage before region-level sensitivity. |
| 4 · 25 Nov | Challenge substantive conclusions. | Baseline, alternative measures, classifications and windows → sensitivity and comparability tables. | Findings and 2–5 | Functional form, influential economies, court proxy, credit outcome, legal coding, cohort and development sensitivities are quantified or their feasibility documented. |
| 5 · 26 Nov | Assemble the argument and verify sources. | Model/diagnostic outputs and primary papers → near-3,000-word report and references. | All | Required sections are ordered, every statistic traces to the R output, the interpretation is associative, and reference metadata and claims are checked. |
| 6 · 27–28 Nov | Prepare the storyboard and close the referee check. | Report and exhibits → storyboard, final clean rebuild, review and corrections. | All | 10–12 slides have takeaways, single exhibits and speaker notes. All outputs agree and the reviewer checks the full methods checklist against the report and appendix. |
| 7 · Follow-up | Explain the initial results as a guided MBA econometrics notebook. | Archived data, audited R stages and the brief → R Markdown source and standalone HTML. | All | Code recreates the figures and tables, hand calculations agree with the fitted models, and the HTML renders offline with working navigation and code disclosure. A fresh-context reviewer checks the methods and interpretation. |
